COMMON QUESTIONS

FAQ

Can I apply right after 10th grade?
Yes — several of our foundation and diploma tracks are open to students straight after 10th grade, with a structured path into specialised programs.
Do you offer placement guarantees?
We offer placement assistance — portfolio review, interview prep and access to our hiring network — rather than a guaranteed job offer.
Can working professionals switch careers here?
Yes, several of our programs are designed for career switchers with flexible batch timings.
Is financial assistance available?
Talk to our counselling team about available fee structures and instalment options for your chosen program.
