Good To Know

Frequently asked
questions.

What courses does Arena Animation offer?+
We offer career-focused programs across Animation, VFX, Gaming, Graphic Design, Web Design and Motion Graphics — from short-term certifications to full diploma tracks, all built around live industry projects.
Do I need a design background to join?+
No prior design experience is required for most of our foundation-level courses. Our mentors start from the fundamentals and build up your skills through hands-on studio work.
How does the placement program work?+
Placement support runs alongside your coursework — resume and portfolio reviews, mock interviews, and direct introductions to our hiring partners as you approach graduation.
Can I attend a demo class before enrolling?+
Yes — we offer a free, no-commitment demo class so you can sit in on a real session, meet the mentors, and see the studio before deciding.
What are the class timings and duration?+
Course duration varies by program, ranging from a few weeks for short certifications to a few years for full diploma tracks. Weekday and weekend batches are both available — ask us for the current schedule.

Ready to build your reel?

Start with a free demo class. No commitment required — come see the studio, meet the mentors, decide from there.
