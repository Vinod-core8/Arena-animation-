Overview

What you'll learn

VFX is a relay race — plate prep, tracking, CGI, simulation and compositing all have to hand off cleanly. You'll learn every leg of it.

✂️

Rotoscoping & Paint

Isolating elements frame-by-frame and cleaning up plates — the unglamorous groundwork every VFX shot depends on.

🎯

Match-Moving & Tracking

Locking CG elements to live-action camera movement with pixel-accurate tracking in Mocha Pro.

🧱

3D & CGI Integration

Building, lighting and rendering 3D assets in Maya so they sit believably inside a live-action plate.

🌊

Simulation & FX

Simulating smoke, fire, water and destruction with Houdini's procedural toolset.

🎞️

Compositing

Layering render passes, plates and effects into a single believable frame in Foundry Nuke.

🎨

Color Grading & Finishing

Matching color and light across every shot so the final sequence feels seamless.

Curriculum

Your VFX pipeline

Tap a stage to see what it covers.

Foundation

Camera fundamentals, image theory and the building blocks of a VFX pipeline — the groundwork before touching production software.

Roto & Prep

Rotoscoping, paint and clean-up work that preps live-action plates for everything that comes downstream.

Tracking

Matching a CG camera to real camera movement in Mocha Pro so digital elements lock convincingly to the plate.

3D & CGI

Modelling, lighting and rendering 3D elements in Autodesk Maya with Arnold/V-Ray so they blend into live-action footage.

Simulation

Houdini-driven simulations — fire, smoke, water, destruction — for the large-scale effects work feature films rely on.

Compositing

Bringing every layer together in Foundry Nuke and Adobe After Effects, then grading the final shot to a portfolio-ready reel.
